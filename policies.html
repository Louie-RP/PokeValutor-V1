Privacy

PokéValutor is designed to work without accounts. By default, your watchlist is stored locally in your browser using local storage.

Local storage

Your watchlist and recent results are saved in your browser (local storage) so they persist between visits. This data stays on your device unless your browser syncs it.

Network requests

When you search, your browser makes requests to the PokéValutor API endpoint (a Cloudflare Worker) which proxies requests to a third-party Pokémon data provider. Those services may receive standard request metadata such as IP address, user agent, and timestamps.
